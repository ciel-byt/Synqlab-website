### Startup Description *

SynqLab is an agentic AI operating system for biological discovery. Scientists describe a goal in plain English — design brighter peptides, better binders, or more stable proteins — and SynqLab coordinates the tools, code, data, and compute behind the scenes. It learns from every experiment, including failures, so each design round improves the next. We are starting with protein and peptide design, then expanding toward fully autonomous design-build-test-learn loops for biotech, pharma, and research teams.

### Anything else we should know?

We have a working platform today: conversational interface, visual pipeline builder, coordinator/worker agent architecture, 43 computational biology tools in the registry, and a closed-loop GFP11 peptide-design workflow. The science is grounded in published work presented at a high-impact AI conference workshop (NeurIPS AI4D3 spotlight) and 113 experimentally labeled GFP11 sequences for training task-specific predictors.
