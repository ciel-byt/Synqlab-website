# team/ — People & collaboration

Who is on the team and how agent work turns into assigned tasks.

## Files


| Path          | Purpose                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `roster.yaml` | Teammates → title, role, reporting line (`reports_to`), areas, and external identities (Linear/Notion/GitHub) |
| `profiles/`   | Resumes & bios, used in decks, applications, and the academic writing                                         |



## Conventions

- Keep `roster.yaml` as the single source mapping a person to their title, reporting line, and external IDs, so org charts and ticket assignment are automatable.
- Don't put confidential personal data here beyond what's needed for presentations.
